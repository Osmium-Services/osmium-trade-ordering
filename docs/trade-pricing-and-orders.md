**Trade Ordering** gives your trade customers their own prices and lets them place orders.

## Per-customer terms

On a trade customer's edit page you can set a **discount** (0 to 100%) and **payment terms** (Cash on Delivery, or 7, 14, 30 or 60 days). With no discount and no terms set, the customer pays shop prices.

## Settings

Open **Trade Ordering** settings.

- **Brands that get the trade discount:** untick a brand to leave it out of every customer's discount. New brands get the discount until you untick them.
- **Discount option prices too:** off, an option costs its shop price on top of the discounted price. On, the discount comes off the option's price as well.
- **Email the customer about their order:** sends a confirmation when they submit and a dispatch notice when you mark the order shipped.
- **Price list categories:** how each shop category appears on the trade price list. **Hidden** also hides the categories inside it.

## Trade orders

**Trade Orders** lists submitted orders, with delivered and cancelled ones in a separate list. Use the row actions to move an order along: **Confirm**, **Mark processing**, **Mark shipped**, **Mark delivered**, **Cancel order**, or **Reopen as pending**. **View details** shows the order, and **Download PDF** produces its document.
